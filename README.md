One sentence, 240 characters at most, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3).
That sentence is the whole entry: no code, no explanation.

## The pitch

> Just tell make.fun the app idea you have been carrying around for years, in plain words, and an AI agent will build the real thing for you — free, no coding, just your idea finally alive in your hands.

201 characters, one sentence.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) holds the pitch as a constant, enforces its own
240-character gate (1–240 characters, not whitespace-only), and writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Deterministic:
every run writes byte-identical output.
